How to play

Around the year 1000 the island is split into eight kingdoms and every lord wants to rule them all, as Brian Boru once did. You win Honour by holding the most raths in kingdoms, by marrying well, by fighting off the Norse and by pleasing the Abbey. Most Honour after the last round wins.

A round

  1. Longship and Match. A longship puts Raiders on the Shore, and a new Match waits at the top of the Suitors’ Ladder. The last round’s Match is always Ragnhild, the Jarl’s daughter.
  2. Draft. You get a pack of deeds. Keep two, pass the rest to the left, and repeat until every deed is taken. (The Quick deal twist skips this.)
  3. Tricks. Whoever holds the 🚩 Banner picks an empty rath and leads a deed of that rath’s colour (or a wild 👑 Crown). Everyone else plays any deed. The highest deed in the rath’s colour, counting Crowns, wins. Then everyone acts from the lowest deed up: the winner uses the deed’s top line (always taking the rath and the Banner), everyone else picks one of its bottom lines. Tricks go on until each lord has one deed left, which is thrown away.
  4. Upkeep. In order: the highest lord on the Ladder makes the Match and drops to the bottom, and everyone else gets the gift on their rung. If any Raiders are left on the Shore, the lord holding the fewest Raiders loses a rath to a Norse ⛵ Longphort. The lord holding the most Raiders gets 1 Glory and then 1 Honour per Glory. The lord with the most Offerings builds a 🏰 Round Tower (the rath counts double) and takes the Banner, and anyone else with 4 or more Offerings builds one too. Last, each kingdom whose seal is open goes to the lord with the most raths there.

Deed symbols

Kingdom seals

Each kingdom shows open-at / worth. Once that many of its raths are filled (by anyone, Norse included), its seal opens. An open seal goes to the lord with the most raths there and only changes hands when someone has strictly more. Round Towers count as two raths.

Final scoring

  • Every seal you hold scores its worth. An open seal still on the board gives half its worth (rounded down) to each lord tied for most raths there.
  • 1 Honour per Glory. 1 Honour for the most Silver (no ties). 1 Honour for holding the Banner.
  • Breadth: raths in 3 to 4 kingdoms score 1, 5 score 3, 6 score 5, 7 score 7, all 8 score 10.
  • Ties go to whoever holds more seals, then more Matches.
