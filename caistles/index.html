Quick game · 3 phases · you vs 2 bots
by OmniXR · myomnixr.com

How to play

You are a noble growing an estate along the river. Over 5 phases of 5 rounds (3 phases in a quick game) you collect hex tiles from the market and lay them into your 37-space estate. Most victory points (VP) at the end wins.

Each round

  • Everyone rolls two dice. The white die decides which depot gets the round's goods tile.
  • Players take turns in turn order. On your turn you use each die for one action, in any order.

The four die actions

  • Take a tile from the depot whose number matches the die. It goes into one of your 3 storage spaces. If storage is full you must throw one stored tile away.
  • Place a tile from storage onto an empty estate space of the same colour that shows the die's number and touches a tile you have already placed. The tile's effect happens straight away.
  • Sell goods: sell your whole stack of the goods type matching the die. You get 1 silver plus 2, 3 or 4 VP per tile (2, 3 or 4 players).
  • Take 2 workers with any die.

Workers: each worker you spend turns a die up or down by 1 (6 and 1 wrap around). Just tap what you want: the game spends the fewest workers needed and shows the cost on the highlight.

Black market: once per turn you may pay 2 silver for a tile from the centre of the market. It goes into storage.

What the tiles do when placed

  • 🏰 Castle (dark green): a bonus action with any number you like.
  • ⛵ Ship (blue): collect all goods from one depot, and move ahead in turn order. You can hold 3 goods types.
  • 🐄 Livestock (light green): score the animals on it, plus all matching animals already in that pasture.
  • 🏠 Building (tan): Market, Carpenter, Chapel (fetch a tile), Warehouse (sell goods), Inn (4 workers), Bank (2 silver), Town Hall (place another tile, any number), Watchtower (4 VP). One of each type per town.
  • ⛏️ Mine (grey): 1 silver at the end of every phase.
  • 📜 Knowledge (yellow): a lasting power or end-of-game points. Tap one to read it.

Scoring

  • Completing an area (all connected spaces of one colour) scores 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28 or 36 VP for sizes 1 to 8, plus the phase bonus: 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 in phases A to E (10, 6, 2 in a quick game).
  • First to fill every space of a colour wins the large colour bonus (5, 6 or 7 VP by player count); second gets the small one (2, 3 or 4).
  • At the end: 1 VP per silver, 1 VP per unsold goods tile, 1 VP per 2 workers, plus knowledge-tile points. Ties go to the player with fewer empty spaces.

Controls

Tap a die to choose it (optional). Tap a highlighted market tile to take it, a stored tile then a highlighted estate space to place it, a goods stack to sell it, or Take 2 workers. Turn on Buy mode to buy from the black market. Tap any player card to look at their estate.
