How to play lAIndlord

Pick a city and race the AI landlords to own its most famous streets. Everyone starts with $1,500. Collect rent, build houses and hotels, make deals, and finish with the most money and property.

Your turn

  • Roll two dice and move clockwise. Passing or landing on 💵 Payday pays you $200.
  • Roll doubles and you go again. Three doubles in one turn and you're busted: straight to 🔒 Lockup.
  • Land on a deed nobody owns and you may buy it at its price. If you pass, it goes to auction and anyone (you too) can bid, starting at $10.
  • Land on someone else's deed and you pay rent. Mortgaged deeds charge no rent.
  • 🍀 Windfall and 📜 City Hall spaces draw a card: cash, bills, trips across town or a Clean Record that gets you out of Lockup.
  • City Tax costs $200 and the Luxury Levy $100. ⛲ City Center is a free rest stop. 🚨 Busted! sends you to Lockup.

Sets, houses and hotels

  • Streets come in 8 color sets, cheapest to most premium: Slate, Seafoam, Citron, Lavender, Copper, Jade, Coral and Gold.
  • Own a whole set and its base rent doubles. Then you can build houses (up to 4) and then a hotel on each street, one at a time and evenly: no street may get two buildings ahead of another in its set.
  • Transit rent is $25, $50, $100 or $200 for 1 to 4 lines. Utilities charge 4× the dice, or 10× if you own both.
  • Need cash? Sell buildings back for half price (evenly), or mortgage an unbuilt deed for half its price. Paying off a mortgage costs that amount plus 10%.

Lockup

In Lockup you can pay $50 bail before you roll, use a Clean Record card, or roll for doubles. Doubles get you out and you move that roll. After three failed tries you pay $50 and move. You still collect rent while in Lockup.

Deals

On your turn, press 🤝 Deal to offer any bot deeds and cash for theirs. Each bot judges the deal by its strategy and accepts, turns it down, or sends a counter-offer you can accept or reject. Bots also offer you deals, and you can accept, turn down or counter once. Deeds in a set with buildings can't be traded until the buildings are sold.

Debt and bankruptcy

If you owe more than you have, sell buildings and mortgage deeds until you can pay. If you still can't, you're bankrupt: your cash and deeds go to the player you owe, or back to the city if you owe the bank.

Ending the game

  • Short game: after the round cap (15, 20 or 30 rounds) the highest net worth wins. Play now starts a 20-round Short game against 2 Medium bots.
  • Net-worth cap: the first landlord to reach the target ($3,000, $4,000 or $6,000) at the end of a turn wins.
  • Both: whichever comes first. Classic: the last landlord not bankrupt wins (after 150 rounds, the highest net worth wins).
  • Net worth = cash + deed prices (half for mortgaged deeds) + what you paid for buildings.

Twists

  • Auctions (on by default): passed deeds go to auction. Off: they stay with the city.
  • Random board order: streets swap places within each price tier.
  • City-center jackpot: taxes, fees and card bills go into a pot. Land on City Center to win it.
  • Speed die: a third die adds 1 to 3 steps, or after your move 🚀 jumps you to the next unowned deed ahead, or 🚌 rides you to the next transit stop. It doesn't count toward doubles.
  • Housing shortage: only 32 houses and 12 hotels exist. When they run out, nobody can build until some are sold.
  • Rubber-banding (on by default): if your net worth is under 70% of the leader's at the start of a turn, you're the trailing landlord. You collect $300 at Payday instead of $200 and pay 20% less rent.

The AI landlords

Each bot plays a named strategy (pick one in Setup or leave it on Random) at Easy, Medium or Hard. Hard bots value sets by how many rounds are left, build to three houses fast and drive a harder bargain.
