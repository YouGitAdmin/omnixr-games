❓ How to play

Everyone starts with a 🌻 Sunflower Patch, a 🥐 Bun Stall and 3 coins. On your turn:

  1. Roll one die (two once you own the 🚋 Tram Depot).
  2. Collect. Every building whose number matches the roll pays out, in this order: red takes coins from the roller, then blue pays its owner on anyone's roll and green pays only on its owner's roll, then purple majors (one of each per city) on your own roll.
  3. Build one building from the market or one landmark, or save your coins.

The first city with all four landmarks wins: 🚋 Tram Depot (4), 🛍️ Neon Plaza (10), 🎡 Sky Wheel (16), 📡 Signal Mast (22).
