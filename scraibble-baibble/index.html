How to play

Drag tiles from your rack onto the board, or tap a tile then tap a square. Tap a tile you placed to take it back. The score preview shows above your rack. Words are checked against the ENABLE word list with 135 slang words removed, plus DA, EE, EW, FE, GI, JA, KI, OI, QI and TE (172,698 words in all). Premium squares move to new spots every game, mirrored the same way on all four sides; the ★ start square stays in the centre. Quick game: 4 rounds each, highest score wins, no penalty for tiles left. Letter values come from Joshua Lewis’s Valett method run on this game’s own word list (Z is 7, W is 3).
