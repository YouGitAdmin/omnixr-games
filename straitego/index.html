How to play

Each side hides its army at its end of the board. You see your own ranks; the bot sees only the backs of yours, and you only the backs of its. Take turns moving one piece. Capture the enemy Banner to win. You also win if the other side has no piece that can move.

  • Moving: one square up, down, left or right. Never diagonally, never into the lakes, never onto your own piece. Traps and the Banner never move.
  • Runners dash any number of empty squares in a straight line, and can attack the piece at the end of the dash.
  • Fights: move onto an enemy piece to attack. Both ranks are shown and the higher rank wins. Equal ranks both fall.
  • The Ghost is the weakest piece, but when it attacks the Titan, the Titan falls. If the Titan attacks first, the Ghost loses.
  • Traps destroy any attacker except a Sapper, which disarms the Trap and takes its square.
  • No shuttling: one piece can't move back and forth between the same two squares more than three times in a row.
  • A dot on an enemy piece means it has moved, so it can't be a Trap or the Banner. 👁 on your piece means the bot has seen its rank.
