How to play TerrAImysticAI

You lead a faction that can only live on one home terrain. Reshape the land around you into that terrain, settle it, grow your settlements into bigger buildings and rise through four Circles. The game lasts 6 rounds (4 in a Quick game, which is what Play now starts). Whoever has the most VP at the end wins.

Each round

  • Income first. Huts pay workers, Markets pay coins and power, Shrines and the Sanctum pay sages. Your Boon and Blessings can add more.
  • Then take turns, one action each, going around the table until everyone has passed. Whoever passes first leads the next round.
  • When the round ends, the round tile rewards your Circle progress (except in the final round), and each Boon nobody took gets an extra coin on it.

What you can do on your turn

  • Reshape and settle: pick a space you can reach and dig it toward your home terrain. Every step around the terrain wheel costs one shovel, and a shovel costs 3 workers until you improve your Digging. Once it is your terrain you may put a Hut on it (usually 1 worker and 2 coins).
  • Reach: you can reach spaces next to your buildings, plus spaces across rivers up to your Sailing level. A bridge you build links both banks for you.
  • Grow a building: Hut → Market (cheaper if a rival lives next door) → either your Citadel (switches on your faction's big ability) or a Shrine → Sanctum. Each Shrine and the Sanctum earn you a Blessing.
  • Improve Sailing (1 sage + 4 coins) or Digging (2 workers + 5 coins + 1 sage). Both stay with you and score VP.
  • Send a sage to a Circle: park it on a free slot for +3 or +2 (the sage stays put), or take a single step and keep the sage.
  • Power actions, each usable once per round by whoever gets there first: Bridge 3⚡, 1 Sage 3⚡, 2 Workers 4⚡, 7 Coins 4⚡, 1 Shovel 4⚡, 2 Shovels 6⚡.
  • Special actions from Boons, Blessings and Citadels work once per round each. Passing scores your Boon, then you trade it in for a fresh one.

Power

Your power tokens flow through three pools. Gaining power first moves tokens from pool I into pool II, then from II into III. Only tokens in pool III can be spent, and spent tokens go back to pool I. In a pinch, sacrifice 2 tokens in pool II to push 1 into pool III (the other is gone for good). Exchange rates: 1⚡ → 1 coin, 3⚡ → 1 worker, 5⚡ → 1 sage, and you can trade a sage for a worker or a worker for a coin. If you come up short when paying, the game makes the exchange for you and says so.

When someone builds next to your buildings, you may draw power equal to the power value of your buildings that touch it (Hut 1, Market or Shrine 2, Citadel or Sanctum 3). It costs you VP equal to that amount minus 1.

Circles, Towns and keys

  • Climbing a Circle to space 3, 5, 7 or 10 pays 1, 2, 2 or 3 power. Space 10 needs a key, and only one player per Circle can get there.
  • When 4 or more of your buildings form one connected group (3 if the Sanctum is in it) with a combined power value of 7 or more, you found a Town. Pick a charter (VP plus a reward) and gain a key.

Final scoring

  • Biggest connected realm (Sailing reach counts): 18 / 12 / 6 VP.
  • Each Circle: 8 / 4 / 2 VP for the leaders (ties share, and nobody scores from space 0).
  • 1 VP for every 3 coins you have left (workers, sages and power in pool III count as coins).
