    How to play

    Everyone gets a secret card, and three more cards sit face-down in the center. At night some roles wake up to look at or move cards. In the morning you talk, then everyone votes at once.

    • Teams: 🐺 the Pack (Wolves and the Lackey), 🏡 the Village (everyone else) and 🎒 the Drifter on its own.
    • You are the card you hold at the end of the night, not the one you started with. Cards can move while you sleep.
    • Voting: the player with the most votes goes out (ties all go out). If nobody gets two or more votes, nobody goes out.
    • The Village wins if at least one Wolf goes out. If no Wolf is among the players, the Village wins when no Village player goes out.
    • The Pack wins if Wolves are among the players and none goes out (and the Drifter survives). If no Wolf is among the players, the Lackey wins when a Village player goes out.
    • The Drifter wins by going out.

    Talking

    Type anything. The bots understand names, role claims (“I’m the Moonseer, I saw FAIng is a Wolf”), accusations (“ShAIdow is lying”), defenses (“I trust LunAI”) and questions (“MAIsie, what’s your role?”, “Who are you voting?”). Tap a seat to mention that bot.
